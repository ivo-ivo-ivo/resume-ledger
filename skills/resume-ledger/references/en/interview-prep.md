# Interview prep (en)

Prep is not a script. It is a three-column table: likely question → answer points → ledger IDs, so every point traces to a fact.

## Format

| Likely question | Answer points | Ledger rows (F ids) |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Questions that must be there
- For every headline number: how it was measured, numerator and denominator, compared with what
- Team and ownership: how many people, what you did yourself, who did the rest
- Every JD requirement marked partial in the hit map: where your boundary is
- Every pilot or planned item: how far it is now
- Every place the resume and the portfolio read differently: how they reconcile
- The biggest mistake; the decision you reversed

## Rules
- Points carry facts and definitions, not phrasing; rehearse phrasing yourself.
- A point with no ledger ID means the fact is not recorded yet; record it first.
- After the interview, write the questions actually asked and the outcome into the registry's feedback column; it is the only feedback loop.
