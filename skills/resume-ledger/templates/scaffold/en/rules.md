# Rules

## Red-line words
Must never appear in anything sent out. Fill in for your industry, employer, region, vendors, personal identifiers. Three placeholders, replace them:
- (example) your legal name
- (example) employer's legal name
- (example) any metric not publicly disclosed

## Banned wordings
- improved by 20% (relative or absolute not stated)
- launched (for a fact whose status is planned)
- built entirely by me (when collaborators existed)

## Allowed wordings
- +20% relative
- +20% YoY / MoM
- I owned X; Y was delivered by Z

## Portfolio de-identification
Never: names, employers, client names, real dates (use relative months), internal version numbers, business-line names or counts, headcounts. Allowed: the AI tools and model vendors you used.
